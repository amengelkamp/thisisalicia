# How to Build a Case Study Page

Derived from the YNAB case study structure. Use these categories as a checklist for every new case study.

---

## 1. Hook / Before-After visual
A single visual that shows the problem and solution in under 2 seconds — no prose required.
- **Question:** What's the one-glance before/after? Pick the single most striking contrast.
- ZooRoyal example: login gate blocking a 12% offer vs. offer landing directly in category

## 2. Context — why this read is different
Your personal angle: why you of all people are the right person to spot this problem.
- **Question:** What's your specific domain overlap with this product? Make it concrete, not generic.
- ZooRoyal example: PO for payment processing at an e-commerce company = you own the layer behind the checkout button

## 3. Problem with social proof
Prove the problem exists beyond your own opinion. Real user voices, stats, or public signals.
- **Question:** Where are users complaining publicly? Trustpilot, App Store, Reddit, forums?
- **Question:** Can you find a number — upvotes, reviews, complaint threads — that anchors the scale of frustration?

## 4. The findings — screen by screen with design rationale
Each finding needs: what the current state is → why it's a problem → what the fix is → why that specific fix.
Not just "what changes" but "why this solution and not another."
- **Question:** For each finding, can you defend the specific improvement you're proposing?
- **Question:** What would a sceptical PM ask, and do you have an answer?

## 5. Mockup / try it yourself
Something visual that shows the improvement — not just describes it.
- **Question:** Can you build a simple before/after HTML mockup, or at minimum an annotated screenshot?
- The more interactive, the better. Even a static side-by-side beats pure text.

## 6. Metrics with targets
Named metrics and specific numbers — not "I'd look at bounce rate" but "bounce rate −10% within 30 days."
- **Question:** For each finding, what is the one metric that proves it worked?
- **Question:** What's the target number, and where does it come from (benchmark, industry data, internal baseline)?

## 7. What's next / roadmap
Show you think beyond the fix. What's the expansion horizon after this lands?
- **Question:** If these findings ship and succeed, what does that unlock?
- **Question:** Is there a bigger UX vision this fits into?

---

## Quick checklist

- [ ] Before/after visual at the top
- [ ] Personal angle explained in 2–3 sentences
- [ ] At least one public signal of the problem (quote, stat, review)
- [ ] Each finding has: current state + problem + fix + rationale
- [ ] At least one visual mockup per finding
- [ ] Every finding has a named metric + target
- [ ] A "what's next" section that goes beyond the immediate fix
