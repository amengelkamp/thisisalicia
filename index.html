What I'm about.

Where I'm from

I grew up in a small village in Germany. Studied sociology and politics in Bielefeld — which taught me more about how systems and people actually work than any product course ever could. My main focus in my studies was quantitative, qualitative research and especially organisational studies.

My path

Sociology degree, web dev bootcamp, three years owning finance systems at one of Germany's largest e-commerce platforms. I got into product from the people side, picked up the technical side along the way, and never lost either.

What I do

I own products end to end — from discovery to shipment. Until recently: invoice creation, payment processing, and finance workflows at Flaschenpost SE. Currently at Ottobock SE, responsible for Content and Experience as a technical product manager.

What I build

I build things that are supposed to work — with technical systems, colleagues and especially customers.
