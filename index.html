What I'm about.

Where I'm from

I grew up in Detmold, a small city in Germany. Studied sociology in Bielefeld — which taught me more about how systems and people actually work than any product course ever could.

My path

Sociology degree, web dev bootcamp, three years owning finance systems at one of Germany's largest e-commerce platforms. I got into product from the people side, picked up the technical side along the way, and never lost either.

What I do

I own products end to end — from discovery to shipment. Most recently: invoice creation, payment processing, and finance workflows at Flaschenpost SE. Business-critical systems where a bug isn't a UX issue, it's a payment failure.

What I build

I build things that are supposed to work — not just look good in a demo.
